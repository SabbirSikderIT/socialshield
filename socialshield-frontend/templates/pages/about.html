🛡️ Our story

A social app for people who don't love socialising

Traditional social apps are built to connect people but rarely help those who fear or avoid social interaction. Social Shield is different.

😣 The problem

Many people feel extreme discomfort at parties, networking events or even casual hangouts. These situations can trigger social anxiety, overstimulation and avoidance.

🌱 Our solution

Social Shield is a smart social assistant tailored for introverts. It helps you:

  • Avoid unwanted interactions with fake calls and exit excuses
  • Feel more confident with conversation starters and skill guides
  • Stay safe with creep alerts and self-defense tutorials
  • Gamify your social progress with 17 cosy games
  • Reflect and track your mood and social battery over time

🗄️ Built on a relational database

Social Shield is a DBMS project. A normalised PostgreSQL database connects 20+ tables: users, trigger zones, goals, events, reviews, creep reports, RSVPs, fake-call scripts, conversation templates, exit excuses, self-defense content, books, skills, games, game progress, mood logs, reflections and chat history.

🧰 Technology

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS (glassmorphism), vanilla JavaScript, Canvas games
  • Backend: Python · Flask · SQLAlchemy
  • Database: PostgreSQL (currently running on {{ db_engine }})
  • AI: Google Gemini powers Shelly, our chatbot
  • Email: Gmail SMTP for password recovery

💜 Values

Introversion is not a flaw. We add humour and gamification to sensitive topics, but always with kindness.
