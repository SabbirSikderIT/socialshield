🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your quiet is sacred, and so is your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email, optional name, city and avatar.
  • Preferences: introversion level, trigger zones and goals.
  • Activity: RSVPs, ratings, creep reports, mood logs, reflections, reading list and game scores.
  • Chat messages you send to Shelly, used to keep the conversation going.

How we use it

Only to run Social Shield's features: personalised suggestions, progress charts and leaderboards. We never sell your data.

Who can see it

Mood logs, reflections and goals are private to you. Ratings show your username; creep reports are anonymous by default. Leaderboards show your username and avatar.

Chatbot

Messages to Shelly are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate replies. Please don't share sensitive personal information in chat.

Passwords

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Reset codes are hashed, expire after 15 minutes and lock after 5 wrong attempts.

Your rights

You can edit your profile at any time and permanently delete your account (and all related data) from your profile page.
